### 高考物理题目

一辆车的初速度为 140 km/h，在 1.8 秒内行驶了 62 米。

**（1）** 如果该车匀减速行驶，它的末端速度是多少？

**（2）** 如果该车允许的减速度最大为 1.1g（约 10.8 m/s²），它的末端**可以达到的**最小速度是多少？

![](data:image/svg+xml;utf8,<svg xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg' width='742' height='598'></svg>)

解答

**第一步：单位换算**

v₀ = 140 ÷ 3.6 ≈ 38.9 m/s

**（1）匀减速情形**

距离和时间固定，平均速度为 ![\bar v = 62 / 1.8 \approx 36.5](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3D%255Cbar%2Bv%2B%253D%2B62%2B%252F%2B1.8%2B%255Capprox%2B36.5) m/s。匀减速下 ![\bar v = (v_0 + v_末)/2](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3D%255Cbar%2Bv%2B%253D%2B%2528v_0%2B%252B%2Bv_%25E6%259C%25AB%2529%252F2)，所以

![v_末 = 2\bar v - v_0 = 2 \times 36.5 - 38.9 = 30.0 \text{ m/s} \approx \mathbf{108 \text{ km/h}}](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3Dv_%25E6%259C%25AB%2B%253D%2B2%255Cbar%2Bv%2B-%2Bv_0%2B%253D%2B2%2B%255Ctimes%2B36.5%2B-%2B38.9%2B%253D%2B30.0%2B%255Ctext%257B%2Bm%252Fs%257D%2B%255Capprox%2B%255Cmathbf%257B108%2B%255Ctext%257B%2Bkm%252Fh%257D%257D)

**（2）末端可以达到的最小速度**

把 1.8 秒拆成**巡航段 + 满刹段**：设满刹时长为 τ，满刹比巡航少走的距离必须等于”滑行富余”：

![\tfrac{1}{2} a_{max} \tau^2 = v_0 t - x = 38.9 \times 1.8 - 62 = 8.0 \text{ 米}](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3D%255Ctfrac%257B1%257D%257B2%257D%2Ba_%257Bmax%257D%2B%255Ctau%255E2%2B%253D%2Bv_0%2Bt%2B-%2Bx%2B%253D%2B38.9%2B%255Ctimes%2B1.8%2B-%2B62%2B%253D%2B8.0%2B%255Ctext%257B%2B%25E7%25B1%25B3%257D)

解得 τ ≈ 1.22 秒（即 0.58 秒巡航 + 1.22 秒满刹）。末端速度：

![v_{末,min} = v_0 - a_{max}\tau = 38.9 - 10.8 \times 1.22 \approx 25.8 \text{ m/s} \approx \mathbf{93 \text{ km/h}}](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3Dv_%257B%25E6%259C%25AB%252Cmin%257D%2B%253D%2Bv_0%2B-%2Ba_%257Bmax%257D%255Ctau%2B%253D%2B38.9%2B-%2B10.8%2B%255Ctimes%2B1.22%2B%255Capprox%2B25.8%2B%255Ctext%257B%2Bm%252Fs%257D%2B%255Capprox%2B%255Cmathbf%257B93%2B%255Ctext%257B%2Bkm%252Fh%257D%257D)

**初速度取其他值时（x = 62 m、t = 1.8 s 不变）：**

  

  

| 初速度 | 滑行富余 | 满刹时长 | 最小末速度 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 130 km/h | 3.0 米 | 0.75 秒 | ≈ 101 km/h |
| 140 km/h | 8.0 米 | 1.22 秒 | ≈ 93 km/h |
| 150 km/h | 13.0 米 | 1.55 秒 | ≈ 90 km/h |

  

### 反直觉之处：初速度越高，末速度反而越低

核心只有两点：

**其一，平均值约束强制两端此消彼长。** 距离和时间钉死，平均速度 ![\bar v = x/t](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3D%255Cbar%2Bv%2B%253D%2Bx%252Ft) 就钉死。匀减速下 ![\bar v = (v_0+v_末)/2](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3D%255Cbar%2Bv%2B%253D%2B%2528v_0%252Bv_%25E6%259C%25AB%2529%252F2)，所以 ![v_末 = 2\bar v - v_0](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3Dv_%25E6%259C%25AB%2B%253D%2B2%255Cbar%2Bv%2B-%2Bv_0)：首速度每抬高一分，末速度就被公式压下一分。这不是车的特性，而是平均值的性质——两端必须关于平均值对称分布。

**其二，”先滑行后刹车”是让高速段占的时间尽可能长。** 固定的 ![\bar v](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3D%255Cbar%2Bv) 本质是按时间加权的速度平均。早刹车会把后面一大段速度拖低，为了守住平均值，刹车只能又轻又短；晚刹车则让高速段占满时间，省出来的”预算”全部换成末尾的满刹——刹得最久，末速度压到最低。

  

### 材料力学题目

一辆质量 3 吨的车以 **110 km/h** 的速度，撞上一个**双肢格构钢柱**（两根钢管由缀条连成的组合柱）侧面。柱的参数：

*   单根钢管截面 **150 mm × 200 mm**，壁厚 **5 mm**，Q355 钢（屈服强度 355 MPa）；
*   两根管的 **200 mm 长边互相平行**（间距方向沿 200 mm 边，好像搞反了，没关系，无伤大雅）；
*   **两管内壁净距 600 mm**（→ 轴线间距 800 mm）；
*   侧面撞击，车头的力分布在 **0.2 m ~ 1 m** 高度的接触带上。

车的溃缩区为 **1.5 m**，车速在溃缩过程中从 110 km/h 匀减速到 0，假设撞击力取平均值。要求：

1.  计算该钢柱根部的**抗弯能力**；
2.  计算撞击对钢柱的作用（剪力、弯矩）；
3.  判断钢柱**能否承受**。

![](data:image/svg+xml;utf8,<svg xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg' width='1548' height='1161'></svg>)

### 解答

### 第 1 问：钢柱的抗弯能力

**① 单杆截面特性：**

![A = 150{\times}200 - 140{\times}190 = 3400\ \text{mm}^2](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3DA%2B%253D%2B150%257B%255Ctimes%257D200%2B-%2B140%257B%255Ctimes%257D190%2B%253D%2B3400%255C%2B%255Ctext%257Bmm%257D%255E2)

![I_0 = \frac{200\times150^3 - 190\times140^3}{12} = 1.28\times10^{-5}\ \text{m}^4](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3DI_0%2B%253D%2B%255Cfrac%257B200%255Ctimes150%255E3%2B-%2B190%255Ctimes140%255E3%257D%257B12%257D%2B%253D%2B1.28%255Ctimes10%255E%257B-5%257D%255C%2B%255Ctext%257Bm%257D%255E4)

**② 移轴合成**（轴线间距 d = 600 + 200 = **800 mm**，间距项占总惯性矩 98%）：

![I = 2\left[I_0 + A\left(\tfrac{d}{2}\right)^2\right] = 1.11\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}^4, \qquad W = \frac{I}{d/2 + B/2} = 2.34\times10^{-3}\ \text{m}^3](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3DI%2B%253D%2B2%255Cleft%255BI_0%2B%252B%2BA%255Cleft%2528%255Ctfrac%257Bd%257D%257B2%257D%255Cright%2529%255E2%255Cright%255D%2B%253D%2B1.11%255Ctimes10%255E%257B-3%257D%255C%2B%255Ctext%257Bm%257D%255E4%252C%2B%255Cqquad%2BW%2B%253D%2B%255Cfrac%257BI%257D%257Bd%252F2%2B%252B%2BB%252F2%257D%2B%253D%2B2.34%255Ctimes10%255E%257B-3%257D%255C%2B%255Ctext%257Bm%257D%255E3)

**③ 抗弯能力（弹性极限）：**

![\boxed{M_{max} = \sigma_s\,W = 355\times10^6 \times 2.34\times10^{-3} \approx 832\ \text{kN·m}}](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3D%255Cboxed%257BM_%257Bmax%257D%2B%253D%2B%255Csigma_s%255C%252CW%2B%253D%2B355%255Ctimes10%255E6%2B%255Ctimes%2B2.34%255Ctimes10%255E%257B-3%257D%2B%255Capprox%2B832%255C%2B%255Ctext%257BkN%25C2%25B7m%257D%257D)

v = 110⁄3.6 = 30.6 m/s，动能 E = ½mv² = **1400 kJ**，匀减速 1.5 m：

![F = \frac{E}{s} = \frac{1.4\times10^6}{1.5} \approx \mathbf{934\ \text{kN}}, \qquad a = \frac{v^2}{2s} \approx 32\,g](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3DF%2B%253D%2B%255Cfrac%257BE%257D%257Bs%257D%2B%253D%2B%255Cfrac%257B1.4%255Ctimes10%255E6%257D%257B1.5%257D%2B%255Capprox%2B%255Cmathbf%257B934%255C%2B%255Ctext%257BkN%257D%257D%252C%2B%255Cqquad%2Ba%2B%253D%2B%255Cfrac%257Bv%255E2%257D%257B2s%257D%2B%255Capprox%2B32%255C%252Cg)

分布力 0.2–1 m 的等效力臂取接触带形心 h = 0.6 m：

![\text{根部剪力 } V = 934\ \text{kN}, \qquad \boxed{M = F \times 0.6 \approx 560\ \text{kN·m}}](https://images.weserv.nl/?url=https%3A//www.zhihu.com/equation%3Ftex%3D%255Ctext%257B%25E6%25A0%25B9%25E9%2583%25A8%25E5%2589%25AA%25E5%258A%259B%2B%257D%2BV%2B%253D%2B934%255C%2B%255Ctext%257BkN%257D%252C%2B%255Cqquad%2B%255Cboxed%257BM%2B%253D%2BF%2B%255Ctimes%2B0.6%2B%255Capprox%2B560%255C%2B%255Ctext%257BkN%25C2%25B7m%257D%257D)
