强答一个，不过我估计这个问题会很快消失。答案也主要以查阅的资料为主，只是列出查阅结果，不带入个人观点。

这个法案涉及的方面很多，包含了对华贸易，科学技术的输出，工业产品的依赖，与台湾的无人技术和[反无人技术](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=762072733&content_type=Answer&match_order=1&q=%E5%8F%8D%E6%97%A0%E4%BA%BA%E6%8A%80%E6%9C%AF&zhida_source=entity)的合作，以及将要在台湾投入的资金等等，就不一一叙述，只作一个简单总结。

**这个方案会实施**。原因很简单——截至 **2025 年 12 月中旬**，**2026 财年《国防授权法案》（FY2026 NDAA）已在美国国会通过并已由总统签署成为法律**，因此其涉华条款不是 “要不要实施” 的政治表态问题，而是进入**行政执行 / 规则制定 / 报告提交 / 资金拨付**等法律实施流程。中国的反对会影响中美关系氛围与美方执行节奏、方式（比如更谨慎、更多豁免 / 延期 / 解释空间），但**不会因为中国反对就 “不实施”**。

下面是一部分别有用心的网友比较关心的问题，关于外国领导人及其家属在美欧财产公布的一项，其结果并不是公布，而是一个评估。

根据目前公开的可靠资料来看，该条款并不是要求 “外国领导人（或其家属）所有在美国的资产登记清单”，而是要求美国[国家情报总监](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=762072733&content_type=Answer&match_order=1&q=%E5%9B%BD%E5%AE%B6%E6%83%85%E6%8A%A5%E6%80%BB%E7%9B%91&zhida_source=entity)（Director of National Intelligence, DNI）编制并向国会提交一份关于**特定外国领导层财富状况的情报评估报告，并在政府网站上公布。**

也就是说：它不是强制公开所有个人资产详细清册，或建立资产业务登记数据库；而是要求情报部门做一个 “公开报告 / 评估”，包括估算财富、金融资产和商业利益等，然后在政府网站上公布。  
这类报告是 “概要型 / 分析型”，不是法律意义上的资产注册表。

这项要求作为法案内容的一部分，已经成为美国法律的一项义务，基本会如期执行，不会因为中国反对或国际压力而被取消或废止。

(As Passed by the House, 09-Sep-2024)

SECTION 1. SHORT TITLE.

This Act may be cited as the “Taiwan Conflict Deterrence Act of 2023”.

SEC. 2. REPORT ON FUNDS OF SENIOR CHINESE OFFICIALS

(a) In General.

(1) Timeline & Trigger.

Not later than 90 days after the date that the President, pursuant to section 3(c) of the [Taiwan Relations Act](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=762382324&content_type=Answer&match_order=1&q=Taiwan+Relations+Act&zhida_source=entity) (22 U.S.C. 3302(c)), informs the Congress of a threat resulting from actions of the People’s Republic of China and any danger to the interests of the United States arising therefrom, and annually thereafter for 3 years, the Secretary of the Treasury shall submit a report to the appropriate Members of Congress containing the following:

(A) With respect to each of at least 10 natural persons described under subsection (b), at least 1 of whom is a natural person listed under paragraph (1) of such subsection (b) and at least 1 of whom is a natural person listed under paragraph (2) of such subsection (b), the estimated total funds that are held in financial institutions and are under direct or indirect control by such natural person and a description of such funds.

(B) A list of any financial institutions that—

(i) maintain an account in connection with significant funds described in subparagraph (A); or

(ii) otherwise provide significant financial services to a natural person covered by the report.

(2) Briefing.

Not later than 30 days after submitting a report described under paragraph (1), the Secretary of the Treasury, or a designee of the Secretary, shall provide to the appropriate Members of Congress an unclassified or classified briefing (as determined appropriate by the Secretary) on the funds covered by the report, including a description of how the funds were acquired, and any illicit or corrupt means employed to acquire or use the funds.

(3) Waiver for Persons/Institutions.

The requirements described under paragraph (1) may not be applied with respect to a natural person or a financial institution, as the case may be, if the President determines:

(A) The funds described under paragraph (1)(A) were primarily acquired through legal and noncorrupt means.

(B) The natural person has agreed to provide significant cooperation to the United States for an important national security purpose with respect to China.

(C) A financial institution has agreed to—

(i) no longer maintain an account described under paragraph (1)(B)(i);

(ii) no longer provide significant financial services to a natural person covered by the report; or

(iii) provide significant cooperation to the United States for an important national security purpose with respect to China.

(4) Presidential Waiver.

The President may waive any requirement described under paragraph (1) with respect to a natural person or a financial institution upon reporting to the appropriate Members of Congress that—

(A) the waiver would substantially promote the objective of ending the threat described under paragraph (1);

(B) the threat described under paragraph (1) is no longer present; or

(C) the waiver is essential to the national security interests of the United States.

(b) Covered Persons.

The natural persons described in this subsection are persons who, at the time of a report, are the following:

(1) A member of the [Politburo Standing Committee](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=762382324&content_type=Answer&match_order=1&q=Politburo+Standing+Committee&zhida_source=entity) of the Chinese Communist Party.

(2) A member of the Politburo of the Chinese Communist Party that is not described under paragraph (1).

(3) A member of the Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party that—

(A) is none of the foregoing; and
